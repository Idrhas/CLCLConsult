About CLCL

Creative Learning Consultancy Limited (CLCL) is a Nigerian educational consultancy service committed to transforming teaching and learning through creativity-driven, student-centred approaches.

We provide training, workshops, curriculum support, classroom innovation strategies, and practical learning resources that help teachers create engaging, meaningful, and enjoyable learning experiences. Our work equips educators with the tools and techniques needed to inspire curiosity, creativity, and independent thinking among students.

CLCL focuses on maximising available resources within schools and communities, making quality education transformation accessible to all — regardless of budget or location.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational consultancy that transforms learning through creativity, innovation, and student-centred teaching practices across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Mission

To empower educators and learners through creative teaching strategies, resource-based learning, and innovative educational practices that develop critical thinkers and effective problem-solvers.
