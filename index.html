Who We Are

Creative Learning Consultancy Limited

We are an educational consultancy dedicated to transforming teaching and learning through creativity-driven, student-centred approaches across Nigerian schools and communities.

Many classrooms today still rely on rote memorisation and passive learning. CLCL bridges the gap between traditional teaching practices and modern creative learning strategies — equipping educators with practical tools to inspire their students.
