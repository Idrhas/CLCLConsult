Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between
Tradition & Innovation

Creative Minds Learning Consultancy (CMLC) is an educational consultancy service designed to transform teaching and learning through creativity-driven and student-centered approaches.

We provide training, workshops, curriculum support, classroom innovation strategies, and practical learning resources that help teachers create engaging, fun, and meaningful learning experiences. Our work equips educators with tools to inspire curiosity, creativity, innovation, and independent thinking among students.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational consultancy that transforms learning through creativity, innovation, and student-centered teaching practices.

Our Mission

To empower educators and learners through creative teaching strategies, resource-based learning, and innovative educational practices that develop critical thinkers and effective problem-solvers.

Our Objectives

  • 01Train teachers on creative and innovative teaching methodologies
  • 02Promote critical thinking and problem-solving skills among students
  • 03Help schools create engaging and enjoyable learning environments
  • 04Support educators in utilizing available resources effectively
  • 05Encourage learner-centered instructional practices
  • 06Develop practical programs that improve student achievement
  • 07Integrate creativity, collaboration, and innovation into classrooms
  • 08Provide educational support for schools, teachers, and parents
