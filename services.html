How We Work

Our Teaching Methodology

All CLCL services are delivered using participatory, evidence-based approaches that put teachers and learners at the centre of the process.

Hands-on activities, real-world challenges, and practical tasks that make abstract concepts concrete and unforgettable for learners of all ages.

Group-based strategies that build teamwork, shared problem-solving, peer learning, and interpersonal communication skills.

Encouraging students to ask bold questions, investigate independently, and develop evidence-based conclusions — just like real scientists and thinkers.

Learning through structured play, games, and activities that make education enjoyable while delivering deep, lasting understanding.

Integrating appropriate digital tools and resources to expand learning beyond the classroom — affordable and adaptable for the Nigerian context.

Using locally available materials, community knowledge, and everyday resources to make learning relevant, affordable, and deeply connected to students' lives.
